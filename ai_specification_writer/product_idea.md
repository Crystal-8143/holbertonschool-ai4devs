# Product Idea

## Product Name
**Carpool Connect**

## Vision
Enable commuters to share rides efficiently, reducing transportation costs, traffic congestion, and carbon emissions while making daily travel more affordable and convenient.

## Users
- **Drivers**: Want to share empty seats in their vehicles, offset fuel costs, and find passengers travelling along similar routes.
- **Passengers**: Want affordable, reliable, and convenient transportation to work, school, or other destinations.
- **Employers and organisations**: Want to encourage carpooling among employees and members to reduce parking demand and support sustainable transportation.

## Key Features
- **Ride Matching**: Match drivers and passengers travelling along similar routes at compatible times.
- **Ride Scheduling**: Allow users to create, search for, and schedule rides by location, date, and time.
- **Cost Sharing**: Help drivers and passengers agree on contributions toward fuel and travel expenses.
- **User Profiles and Ratings**: Allow users to create profiles and rate their experiences to encourage trust and accountability.
- **Ride Requests and Confirmations**: Enable passengers to request seats and drivers to accept or decline requests.
- **Trip Notifications**: Notify users about ride confirmations, schedule changes, and upcoming trips.
- **Eco-Impact Tracking**: Estimate the environmental benefits of sharing rides instead of travelling separately.

## Differentiators
- **Convenient Ride Matching**: Focus on matching commuters based on their routes and schedules.
- **Affordable Transportation**: Help users reduce individual commuting costs through cost sharing.
- **Safety and Trust**: Provide profiles, ratings, and clear trip details to help users make informed decisions.
- **Environmental Awareness**: Highlight estimated carbon emissions avoided through carpooling.
- **Simple User Experience**: Make finding, offering, and managing rides straightforward for users of different technical skill levels.
