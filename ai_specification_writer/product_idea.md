# Product Idea

## Product Name
**Job Tracker**

## Vision
Help job seekers organise their job search in one simple place, making it easier to track applications, remember follow-up actions, and understand their progress toward finding a role.

## Users
- **Job seekers**: Need a clear overview of the jobs they have applied for, the stage each application is at, and what they should do next.
- **Students and recent graduates**: Need a straightforward way to manage multiple applications while learning how to navigate the job market.
- **Career changers**: Need to organise applications across different companies and roles without losing track of important details.

## Key Features
- **Application tracking**: add jobs with company, role, link, location, salary (if available), and date applied
- **Status pipeline**: move applications through stages (Saved, Applied, Interview, Offer, Rejected)
- **Deadlines and reminders**: never miss an application deadline, interview, or follow-up date
- **Notes and contacts**: store interview notes, recruiter details, and job description highlights per application
- **Search, filter, and sort**: quickly find jobs by status, company, or date
- **Dashboard and statistics**: view totals, response rate, and interview rate at a glance
- **Local data persistence**: saved in the browser, so data stays after closing the page

## Differentiators
- **Simple and distraction-free**: no account or setup required, just open and start tracking
- **Privacy-first**: all data stays on the user's device
- **Action-focused**: highlights what needs attention next, rather than being just a static list
- **Insight into the search**: statistics help users see what is working and adjust their approach
- **Lightweight and fast**: built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no heavy dependencies.
